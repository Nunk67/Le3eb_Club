# Policy Cost Rules (Rule)

> CORE-ALGO-MARKER: Policy cost tiers directly affect payout and risk accounting.
> Any tier change must keep POL validation tokens and mapping synchronized.

## Cost tiers

Core companion revenue share bands include **40%** as the documented baseline for the lowest tier in this harness snapshot.

## Exclusions

**Explicitly excluded** from automatic policy-cost inclusion: **guild monthly bonus** pools (tracked separately; do not merge into hourly settlement tiers).
